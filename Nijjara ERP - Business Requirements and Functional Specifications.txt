【 CONCEPT 】
━━━━━━━━━━━━
This document describes, in clear business language, what the Nijjara ERP must do for the company. It answers what we expect the system to support day-to-day across four areas:
- Project Management (PRJ)
- Finance (FIN)
- Human Resources (HRM)
- System Administration (SYS)
It focuses on business needs, operations, roles, rules, KPIs, integrations, reporting, and the information we manage. No technical implementation details are included.
============================================================
1.0 BUSINESS DATA STRUCTURE
============================================================
We manage well-defined business records. For each record type, we agree on:
- A clear, human-readable identifier (e.g., PRJ-1001).
- The information shown in lists versus what appears in detailed views.
- The Arabic labels visible to staff.
- Which fields are mandatory for data quality.
Records remain stable after go-live. Administrators may adjust labels or what appears in lists without altering the meaning of the records.
============================================================
2.0 OPERATING CONTEXT
============================================================
- Language: All staff-facing content is Arabic.
- Access: Staff see only the tools and data relevant to their roles.
- Usability: Screens are consistent, simple, and support quick data entry and review.
- Governance: All actions are traceable and auditable.
============================================================
3.0 CORE BUSINESS PROCESSES
============================================================
The system supports end-to-end business workflows across modules.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3.1 Access & Roles
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Staff sign in with their company accounts.
- Based on role, each person sees their module menus, lists, and actions.
- Sensitive actions (approvals, payroll, allocations) require appropriate roles.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3.2 Human Resources (HRM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Departments: Create, update, and list departments with active/inactive status and sort order.
- Employees: Add and maintain complete employee profiles (personal, job, financial, contact, emergency).
- Attendance: Record daily check-in/out times, late/early minutes, and overtime minutes.
- Leave: Request, approve/reject, and record leave with start/end dates and number of days.
- Advances: Issue, track, and settle employee advances.
- Overtime: Record overtime entries and associated amounts.
- Deductions: Record penalties or deductions and link to payroll.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3.3 Projects (PRJ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Clients: Maintain client records and their contacts.
- Projects: Create projects with name, client, type/status, budget, planned start, and location.
- Tasks: Create and assign tasks to employees; track status, dates, and priority.
- Materials: Maintain a price list and classifications for materials.
- Plan vs Actual: Track planned versus actual timelines and costs for governance.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3.4 Finance (FIN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Direct Expenses: Record project-linked purchases and payments with quantities, prices, totals, VAT, notes, and payment status/method.
- Indirect Expenses (Time-Based): Record time-bound indirect costs; allocate fairly across active projects based on overlapping days.
- Indirect Expenses (Fixed/Budget-Based): Record fixed indirect costs; allocate fairly based on the relative budgets of active projects.
- Revenue: Record project revenue with type, source, invoice number, payment status, totals, and remaining amounts.
- Custody: Track employee custody balances linked to projects and purposes.
- Payroll: Generate payroll periods and net pay considering salary, overtime, advances, and deductions.
- Profit & Loss: Summarize revenue and expenses over a period into statements.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3.5 System Administration (SYS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Users & Roles: Maintain user accounts and roles; define what each role can do.
- Permissions: Manage granular permissions and link them to roles.
- Sessions: Track user sessions for accountability.
- Public Holidays: Maintain a calendar of official holidays.
- Documents: Link uploaded files to their business records for quick access.
- Audit Log: Record who did what, when, and on which record.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3.6 Business Rules & Decision Logic
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Identifiers: Use sequential, readable IDs with module prefixes (e.g., HRM-1001).
- Mandatory Data: Certain fields must be completed before saving.
- Lookups: When choosing an existing person or project, search by name, email, or phone and select the correct record.
- Allocations (Time-Based): Share an indirect cost across projects in proportion to overlapping days.
- Allocations (Budget-Based): Share an indirect cost across projects in proportion to their budgets.
- Approvals: Actions like leave and payroll require designated approvers.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3.7 Key Performance Indicators & Success Metrics
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Overall:
  - Time to complete common actions (login, save, approval).
  - Percentage of actions blocked correctly due to insufficient permissions.
  - Percentage of successful saves without validation errors.
- HRM:
  - Employee profile completeness.
  - Attendance compliance (days with valid check-in/out).
  - Payroll accuracy (net pay matches expected rules).
- PRJ:
  - On-time project start rate.
  - Budget variance from plan.
  - Task completion by due date.
- FIN:
  - Lead time from expense request to posting.
  - Revenue collection cycle time.
  - Allocation accuracy versus expected method.
- SYS:
  - Audit coverage of actions.
  - Stability of active sessions.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3.8 Reporting & Analytics Needs
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Dashboards per module with Arabic labels.
- Standard Reports:
  - HRM: Employee roster, attendance summary, leave history, payroll statements.
  - PRJ: Project portfolio, plan vs actual, tasks by owner, materials list.
  - FIN: Direct and indirect expenses, revenue ledger, custody balances, profit & loss statements.
  - SYS: Audit log extracts, session summaries, role/permission matrices.
- Filters by date ranges, department, job title, project, and status.
- Printable and exportable summaries for reviews and governance.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3.9 Integrations
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Company Email: Password reset messages, approvals, and alerts.
- Document Storage: Store and retrieve attachments linked to records.
- Accounting System: Export financial postings and statements.
- Analytics/BI: Share data for dashboards and KPIs.
All integrations must protect data integrity, respect roles, and remain auditable.

4.0  >>  BUSINESS RECORDS & INFORMATION FLOWS
================================================================================
We maintain the following records. Each record includes essential business information, creator/updater details, and timestamps. Information flows from data entry to review, approval (where applicable), and reporting.
[ System Administration (SYS) ]
- Users: Name, username, email, job title, department, status, last login, created/updated by.
- Roles: Title, notes, whether a role is system-level.
- Permissions: Name, category, notes, created/updated by.
- Role Permissions: Role, permission, scope, allowed/blocked, constraints.
- Sessions: Session type/status, start/end times, IP address, actor.
- Public Holidays: Date and name.
- Documents: Linked entity and ID, file name, label, storage link, uploaded by, created at.
- Audit Log: Actor, action (add/update/delete), description, entity, entity ID, timestamp, scope.
[ Human Resources (HRM) ]
- Departments: Name, active status, sort order.
- Employees: Names (Arabic/English), birth date, gender, nationality, marital/military status, contacts, address, email, job title, department, hire date, contract type, salary, allowances, deductions.
- Attendance: Date, check-in/out, late/early minutes, overtime minutes, notes, status.
- Leave: Type, start/end, number of days, approved by, notes.
- Advances: Issue date, amount, settlement period, notes, status.
- Overtime: Policy reference, date, minutes, amount.
- Deductions: Penalty reference, employee, date, amount.
[ Projects (PRJ) ]
- Clients: Name, phones, email, created/updated by.
- Projects (Main): Name, client, status, type, budget, planned start, location, created/updated by.
- Tasks: Name, priority, assigned employee, plan/start/end dates, status, created/updated by.
- Materials: Name, category/subcategories, default unit, active status, price list details.
- Plan vs Actual: Planned and actual dates, number of days, direct expense totals, materials totals.
- Indirect Expense Allocations (Time & No-Time): Allocation method, amount, project, timestamps.
[ Finance (FIN) ]
- Direct Expenses: Project, date, material, unit, price, quantity, totals (VAT excl/inc), payment status/method, notes.
- Payroll: Employee, period start/end, basic salary, overtime total, advance instalments, deduction total, net pay, status.
- Indirect Expenses (Time): Category, subcategory, start/end, payment status/method, notes.
- Indirect Expenses (No-Time): Category, subcategory, useful life (months), depreciation start date, payment status/method, notes.
- Profit & Loss: Revenue total, direct expenses total, indirect expenses totals, start/end dates.
- Revenue: Project, date, amount, type, source, payment method, invoice number, payment status, total/remaining.

5.0 REFERENCE LISTS & ACTIONS
============================================================
Reference Lists (Dropdowns): Central list of selectable options such as gender, project status, payment methods, expense categories, and job titles in both Arabic and English, with active flags and sort order.
Action Screens (Forms): Named screens to add or edit records (e.g., Add User, Add Employee, Add Project, Add Direct Expense, Add Payroll).
List Screens (Views): Named lists to review records with filters (e.g., Users, Employees, Attendance, Projects, Tasks, Expenses, Revenue, Payroll).
Action Buttons: Named actions like Save, Approve, Reject, Edit, Deactivate, Mark as Done.

6.0 WALK-THROUGH EXAMPLE
============================================================
Scenario: HR Officer signs in → Views Employees → Adds New Employee → Saves → Sees Updated List.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Access
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The HR Officer signs in and is shown HR tools and lists appropriate to the role.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
6.2 Navigate to Employees
--------------------------------------------------------------------
From the HR menu, the officer opens the Employees list and sees key details in Arabic.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
6.3 Add New Employee
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The officer clicks “إضافة موظف جديد” and a form opens with tabs for basic information, job, and contact.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
6.4 Fill Details
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Examples: employee name, job title, department, email, mobile, hire date, basic salary.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
6.5 Save
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The system checks required fields, assigns the next employee ID, saves the record, writes an audit entry, and shows a success message in Arabic.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
6.6 List Updates
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The Employees list refreshes automatically and shows the new record.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
6.7 View & Edit
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The officer opens the employee’s details. If permitted, the “تعديل” action appears. Example: update salary with the change recorded in the audit log.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
6.8 Sign Out
--------------------------------------------------------------------
When finished, the officer signs out and the session ends.

7.0 OPERATIONAL LOGS & MONITORING
============================================================
We maintain three logs to support governance and troubleshooting:
--------------------------------------------------------------------
# 7.1 Application Log
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Records successful events and routine operations with timestamps, user names, actions, and context.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
# 7.2 Warning Log
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Captures potential issues (validation failures, blocked access attempts, slow operations) to enable proactive attention.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
# 7.3 Error Log
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Captures critical failures with error details and the action that caused them. This is the first place to inspect when a problem is reported.
==> Operational Behavior
----> Trigger: When staff perform actions, the system records key details.
----> Processing: Actor, action, record type, record ID, and timestamp are captured.
----> Storage: Each log is maintained for filtering and analysis as needed.
